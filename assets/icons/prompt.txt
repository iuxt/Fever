Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: final cross-platform desktop application icon for Fever, a local-first Markdown notes app, for macOS Dock, Windows taskbar and Linux desktop.
Primary request: Design one exceptionally polished restrained skeuomorphic app icon. A compact warm burnt-orange leather notebook with rounded corners, facing almost straight forward, only slight depth at the right and bottom edge. The notebook itself is the icon silhouette, no separate background tile. A bold simple flame emblem is debossed into the center of the leather cover, tactile and clearly readable, evoking the app name Fever and the spark of writing. A narrow ivory paper edge at the right, and one short dark terracotta ribbon bookmark at the bottom. Elegant subtle leather grain, understated stitching very close to the perimeter, softly bevelled edges. Rich realistic materials but clean iconic geometry, not a busy photoreal scene. Flame is one simple recognizable silhouette, no text or letterforms.
Composition/framing: square 1024x1024 canvas, single centered icon occupying approximately 82 percent of canvas width and 86 percent height, consistent transparent padding, all corners and shadow fully within the canvas. Broad strong silhouette and central flame remain legible at 32 pixels.
Lighting/mood: soft upper-left studio key light, controlled highlights, subtle dimensional contact shadow immediately below the object. Warm, calm, crafted productivity aesthetic.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background.
Constraints: one icon only, no presentation board, no grids, no alternate versions, no lettering, no watermark, no surrounding objects, no pencil, no OS logos, no glossy plastic, no chrome frame, no exaggerated perspective, no diffuse glow. The notebook cover has gentle rounded-square proportions. The embossed flame must have clear tonal contrast against the orange leather.
